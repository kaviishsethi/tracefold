## Partner handoff procedure

The Northstar Demo partner pilot uses the internal project label Lantern Orchard.
Before a partner receives a sandbox export, the integration owner must replace
customer names with invented labels and remove free-text support notes. The
reviewer records the export under batch reference ND-742-MAPLE and checks that
only the demonstration inventory remains. If a shipment includes the blue
compass marker, pause the handoff and request a second review from the pilot
coordinator. The partner receives the approved export through the sandbox portal,
while the review checklist remains in the internal workspace.

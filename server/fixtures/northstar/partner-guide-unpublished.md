# Northstar Demo — unpublished onboarding note

This fictional onboarding exercise gives each partner an empty practice account.
An administrator schedules a walkthrough, demonstrates the sample dashboard,
and asks the participant to confirm that the example reports are understandable.
Feedback is collected in a local worksheet before the next training session.
No production records are used in the exercise. Access expires after the practice
session, and the administrator resets the account before another participant joins.

# All-hands follow-up

Team,

Thank you for the questions after this week's meeting. Several of you asked how we will handle the issues raised, so here is a short summary.

First, on the topic we discussed at length: We also know that people who leak confidential information do not belong here.

Second, leadership will publish an updated plan next month and assure you that we are doing everything in our power to identify those who leaked.

Please send further questions to your manager or the internal feedback form.

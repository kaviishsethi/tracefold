# Follow-up from Monday's all-hands

Hi team,

A few of you asked for the main points from Monday in writing, so here they are.

The headline is the one you already heard: we will gradually stop using contractors to do work that AI can handle. Each function lead will share a timeline with their teams over the next few weeks, and nobody should hear about a change to their own work from someone outside their team.

We know this will mean we need to rethink much of how we work. That is uncomfortable, and it is fine to say so in your 1:1s.

The reason matters more than the policy. AI isn't just a productivity boost; it helps us get closer to our mission.

Please keep this note inside the company. Questions go to your manager or the all-hands form.
